Marketing philosophy

Why this content?

Audiences are tired of promises. The real differentiator isn’t advertising — it’s education.

PRINCIPLE 01

Educate, don’t sell

Every piece of content teaches something real. The audience feels you give value before asking for anything.

PRINCIPLE 02

Honesty builds trust

No guarantees, no exaggeration. When we say “the outcome depends on your case” — that’s what makes people trust us.

PRINCIPLE 03

Clarity is the service

Explaining a complex term in plain language is a real service the content delivers before any consultation.

PRINCIPLE 04

Faces build relationships

Samah appears in the content. A human face creates emotional connection that a logo never can.

PRINCIPLE 05

Consistency over perfection

Consistent good-quality posting beats sporadic high-quality posting.

PRINCIPLE 06

Compliance is non-negotiable

Every post goes through Samah before publishing. Protecting the licence matters more than any marketing result.

Compliance

CICC rules — non-negotiable

Samah is regulated by CICC, and all marketing must follow the Code of Professional Conduct. These are general rules, and Samah approves every post before publishing.

✕ Strictly prohibited

  • Guaranteeing or implying approval, a result, or a timeline
  • Superlatives like “best” or “number one”
  • Using “fast” or “efficient” as a promise of speed or success
  • Using a title Samah does not hold
  • Publishing fees, processing times, or program rules without confirmation
  • Client names, file numbers, or identifiable details
  • Describing team members as consultants
  • Naming partner organizations in public marketing

✓ Always required

  • Using “Regulated Canadian Immigration Consultant (RCIC)” with #R709587
  • Samah’s approval on every post, ad, and page before publishing
  • Samah’s review of every Arabic text before publishing
  • Accuracy and currency — no outdated information
  • Client confidentiality in all content
  • Samah’s approval on any testimonial or client story
  • Describing the team by their real roles only
  • Professional translation and approval for any French content
